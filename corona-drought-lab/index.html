How the brand sensitivity is calculated

The exploratory coefficient (λ) is 0.023, from 11 matched annual observations over 2015–2025. Its 95% interval spans −0.792 to 0.838 and includes zero. Conditional holdouts were within 10% in 2 of 5 years, versus 3 of 5 using the prior year’s value. This does not establish a reliable forecast or water causality. Zero selected sensitivity does not establish zero real risk.

Brand reduction = USD 9.7bn × [1 − (1 − allocated annual EBIT loss / USD 15.405bn)^λ]. Earnings pressure is capped at 95%, with a warning when triggered. Only selected calendar-year contribution loss enters; protection expenses are excluded. Brand-value stocks are never added to annual costs or summed across months or years. Group earnings are broader than Corona; replacement production and reputation effects remain uncalibrated.

All assumptions, company accounts and remaining data needs

Current storage = 3 demand-days. The combined option adds 7 storage days and up to 20% alternative daily supply. All buffers start full. Storage carries across months; it refills only when supply exceeds use. Supply returns to 100% for the three recovery months. Demand and linear water-to-output response have no seasonality; essential non-production water = 0%; sales substitution = 0%.

Alternative-water expense at full capacity = 2% of affected normal monthly sales, charged for capacity used. Additional storage service expense = 0.1% of affected normal monthly sales throughout the scenario. No upfront CapEx is estimated. Fixed costs remain. Lost sales × contribution margin + additional protection expenses = operating-profit reduction. Calendar days and leap years apply; no event probability, recurrence or annualization is assigned.
